Slide the tiles – they all move at once, and equal ones merge into one. The rules take a second to learn; the depth shows up when the board stops forgiving a careless move.

The game’s palettes

Three ways to merge

Each rule set changes what you look for on the board. The habits you build in one get in the way in the next.

  • 22→4

    Powers of two

    The classic: two equal tiles become one, worth their sum.

  • 333→9

    Powers of three

    Three equal tiles become one. More room on the board, and every mistake costs more.

  • 35→8

    Fibonacci

    Neighbours in the sequence merge, not equal tiles: 3 and 5 make 8.

Etudes: a set position, a goal, a few moves

Make 128 in four moves, or leave just five tiles on the board. No new tiles appear, so everything you need is already there – and every etude has an exact solution.

  • 84 etudes in seven chapters, from 3×3 to Fibonacci on 5×5
  • A hint shows the first move; after a miss, the game replays the solution
  • A free etude of the day for everyone, longer towards the end of the week

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone

One board for every player and a single attempt: no restart, no undo. Share your result as a card and keep your streak of solved days going.

Challenge a friend

Send a link to your deal. Your friend plays the very same board and sees the score to beat – even without the game installed, right from the link.

Help when you’re stuck, and a look back when it’s over

Hints

Stuck? Tap the light bulb and an arrow suggests a move. Three hints per game, free; the daily puzzle and challenges stay hint-free, since everyone plays them with a single attempt.

Game review

After a game the app replays it and finds the move where it went wrong: what you played, and what would have been better. It all runs on your device.

Five boards, eight palettes

From a cramped 3×3, where every move counts, to a roomy 7×7. Each rule set and size keeps its own record and its own leaderboard.

Five palettes come with the game, three more with Tilefold Plus. The numbers on every tile are picked for contrast, so the board stays readable in any of them.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The game asks for nothing about you. Records and statistics go to your own iCloud. To fix crashes and see which screens are used, anonymous crash reports and usage statistics are sent to Google Firebase – no advertising identifier, nothing that identifies you.

Questions

Is Tilefold free?
Yes. Every rule set, board size, the daily puzzle, challenges, hints and the etude of the day are free. Tilefold Plus is an optional one-time purchase.
Are there ads?
No. There are no ads in the game, no advertising identifier and no tracking across apps and websites.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 26 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon.
Will my records move to a new device?
Yes. Records, statistics and daily puzzle results are kept in your iCloud and arrive on any device signed in to the same account.
I bought Plus – how do I get it on another device?
Open Settings › Tilefold Plus and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Only Game Center and syncing through iCloud need a connection, and the game works fine without them.
